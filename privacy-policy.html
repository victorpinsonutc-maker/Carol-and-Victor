Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Carol and Victor – DisplateAI Factory is a private content publishing and analytics application used to manage content on our own Pinterest business account.

Information We Access

Through the Pinterest API, the application may access information associated with our authenticated Pinterest account, including Pins, boards, Pin performance data, impressions, saves, clicks, and other analytics made available through the Pinterest API.

How We Use Information

The information is used exclusively to:

  • Create and publish our own Pinterest content
  • Manage Pins and boards
  • Measure content performance
  • Analyze impressions, engagement, saves, and outbound clicks
  • Improve our artwork and content strategy

Third-Party Data

The application is not intended to access or manage Pinterest accounts belonging to other users. It is used for our own authenticated Pinterest account.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information or Pinterest user data. Data obtained through the Pinterest API is not shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Data Storage and Security

Any data retrieved through the Pinterest API is used only for the operation and analytics of the application. Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored application data and API credentials.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained only for as long as necessary for content management and performance analysis. Requests concerning deletion of data associated with the application can be submitted using the contact information below.

Third-Party Services

The application uses the Pinterest API and may link to external product pages. Use of Pinterest is also subject to Pinterest's own terms and privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data practices change.
