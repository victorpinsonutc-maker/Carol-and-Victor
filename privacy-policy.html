Privacy Policy

CYCLAZ

Last updated: September 13, 2026

CYCLAZ is a private creative automation, content publishing and analytics platform used to manage and evaluate original digital content across supported online platforms.

This Privacy Policy explains how CYCLAZ accesses, uses and protects information when interacting with third-party services, including Pinterest.

Information We Access

When CYCLAZ is connected to an authenticated Pinterest account through the Pinterest API, it may access information made available by Pinterest and authorized by the account owner.

This may include Pins, boards, Pin and board identifiers, publishing information, and performance or analytics data such as impressions, saves, engagement and outbound clicks.

How We Use Information

Information accessed through connected services is used only for operating CYCLAZ and its content workflows.

This includes creating and publishing owned content, organizing Pins and boards, measuring content performance, analyzing engagement and outbound clicks, and improving future creative and publishing decisions.

Private Use

CYCLAZ is currently a private platform intended to manage content and accounts owned or operated by its owner. It is not currently offered as a public service for third-party users.

Information Sharing

CYCLAZ does not sell personal information or Pinterest account data. Information obtained through connected services is not sold to advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Information may only be processed by service providers when technically necessary to operate the platform, or disclosed when required by applicable law.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect credentials, authentication tokens and information processed by CYCLAZ against unauthorized access, disclosure or misuse.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the platform, analyze content performance, maintain required records or comply with applicable obligations.

Data Deletion

Data associated with a connected service may be deleted when it is no longer required or when access to that service is disconnected.

Requests concerning data associated with CYCLAZ can also be submitted using the contact information below.

Third-Party Services

CYCLAZ may interact with third-party platforms such as Pinterest and external product or content pages. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as CYCLAZ evolves or as its integrations and legal requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
